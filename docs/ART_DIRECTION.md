# Port Meridian art direction

Port Meridian is a navigable combat space, not an anonymous block scatter: Harbour Reach marks the western coast, Northbank the northern skyline, and Meridian Core the twin central landmarks. The 550m and 490m towers have three real collision tiers, contrasting ivory crowns, and warm occupied windows. Road axes form broad linked combat corridors; the waterfront provides a low escape route. Roof pads and equipment markings stay on surfaces so a cosmetic solid never lies outside its collision envelope.

Falcon uses a narrower swept-wing planform, long interceptor nose, visible intake lips, canted fins and underwing stores. Wraith uses a broad faceted delta, darker panels and twin exhausts. Both are original procedural models. Orange exhaust and restrained navy/ivory/orange flight instruments establish a consistent identity. No downloaded models, GTA assets, stock city meshes or image-generated placeholders are used.
